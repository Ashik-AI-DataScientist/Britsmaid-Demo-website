Discover Kumbalangi

Escape the busy city and experience the authentic beauty of Kerala. Kumbalangi is known for its peaceful backwaters, coconut palms, fishing villages, traditional Chinese fishing nets and relaxed village atmosphere.

Explore Kochi

Divya Villa is a peaceful base for exploring Kochi’s popular attractions.

• Fort Kochi — Historic streets, cafés and Chinese fishing nets.

• Mattancherry Palace — Kochi’s rich history and architecture.

• Jew Town & Paradesi Synagogue — Historic streets, antique shops and boutiques.

• Marine Drive — Waterfront views and evening walks.

• Kerala Folklore Museum — Traditional art, culture and architecture.

• Cherai Beach — A relaxing day by the sea.

• Kochi City Centre — Shopping, restaurants and entertainment.

Perfect for families and groups

With four bedrooms and four bathrooms, Divya Villa is ideal for families and groups who prefer staying together in a private home rather than separate hotel rooms.

Whether you’re visiting Kochi for sightseeing, a family holiday, a wedding, a special occasion or simply a peaceful break, Divya Villa offers a comfortable and relaxing base to experience Kochi and Kumbalangi. We look forward to welcoming you to Divya Villa and Kerala.

Guest access

Guests have exclusive access to the entire private villa, including all four bedrooms, bathrooms, living areas, dining area, kitchen and outdoor spaces. Private parking is available at the property.

Enjoy complete privacy throughout your stay. Access details, including key or lockbox instructions, will be shared before check-in.
