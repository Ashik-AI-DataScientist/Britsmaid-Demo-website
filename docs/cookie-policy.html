Cookie Notice

Draft — last reviewed October 2026

This notice describes the current preview. Review it against the final hosting and integrations before launch.

What cookies we use

Essential session: the website uses a cookie named session for admin sign-in and protection against unauthorised feedback and booking submissions. Public forms obtain it when a submission is started. It lasts up to eight hours for a signed-in admin session; public request sessions normally end when the browser session closes. It is not an advertising cookie.

Analytics and advertising: this preview does not install Google Analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. If optional tracking is introduced, this notice and the consent controls must be updated before it is enabled.

External services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When an online owner enquiry is submitted, the configured delivery integration may load a Google Apps Script frame. Google controls its own service behaviour. WhatsApp and email buttons open the service or application you choose; their own privacy terms apply.

Managing your preferences

You can remove or block cookies through your browser settings. Removing the admin session cookie signs you out of the content studio. The current preview has no optional analytics preference to enable.

Your preferences: a notice appears on your first visit. Choose “Use essential only” or open Cookie settings. You can reopen settings from the footer. Your preference is stored in this browser’s local storage for 180 days; this storage is used only to remember your privacy choice. Analytics and advertising options are disabled because those trackers are not installed.
