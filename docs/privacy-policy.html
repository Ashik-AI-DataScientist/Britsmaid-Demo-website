Privacy Policy

Draft — last reviewed October 2026

This is a placeholder draft for prototype purposes. It must be reviewed and finalised by a qualified legal professional before the site goes live, particularly regarding DPDP Act 2023 (India) compliance for personal data collected through enquiry forms.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form (List Your Property, Sell Your Property, or general contact), we collect the information you provide: name, contact details, property details, and any message or documents you choose to share.

Booking enquiries also include your chosen property, dates, guest count, notes and consent. They are saved for our team to review. WhatsApp and email buttons prepare a message in your chosen app; you decide whether to send it. Those services process messages under their own privacy terms.

Feedback includes your display name, experience type, comments and any optional property or reply email. Feedback is private until reviewed. We request separate permission to display your name and comments publicly; your contact email is not displayed.

Submitting the revenue calculator, partnership or tailored-proposal form saves your name, phone or email, property answers, illustrative scenario and consent in our private CRM. Our team records follow-up notes and enquiry progress.

If you choose to provide check-in registration with a booking enquiry, we also collect guest names, whether each guest is under 18, selected relationships, parent/legal guardian details for minors and private ID images. Authorised staff review these details manually. ID images are encrypted in storage and excluded from public pages and WhatsApp/email enquiry messages. They are normally removed 30 days after checkout; for unconfirmed or cancelled enquiries, they expire after 30 days. Booking records and staff review notes are retained separately. Contact us for correction or deletion requests.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, assess your property, and — if you proceed — to manage the relationship between you and Britsmaid. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Cookies and analytics

The website uses an essential session cookie for admin sign-in and to protect feedback and booking submissions against unauthorised requests. The current preview does not install Google Analytics or advertising trackers. See our Cookie Notice for details.

Data storage and security

Enquiry data is stored securely and is not publicly accessible. Access is limited to authorised Britsmaid staff.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details on our Contact page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact Britsmaid Property Management Kerala Pvt. Ltd. via the details in the site footer.
