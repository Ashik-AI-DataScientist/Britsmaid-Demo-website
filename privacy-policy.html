What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form (List Your Property, Sell Your Property, or general contact), we collect the information you provide: name, contact details, property details, and any message or documents you choose to share.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, assess your property, and — if you proceed — to manage the relationship between you and Britsmaid. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies for essential site functionality and, where you consent, for analytics (Google Analytics) to understand how the site is used. See our Cookie Notice for details.

Data storage and security

Enquiry data is stored securely and is not publicly accessible. Access is limited to authorised Britsmaid staff.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details on our Contact page.
