cms            Backup, export and publishing tools.
tests          Current automated checks.
docs           Generated GitHub Pages demonstration.
deliverables   Finished business reports and documents.
previews       Website screenshots, kept out of GitHub.
archive        Older checks and integration screenshots, kept out of GitHub.
.data          Private database, uploads and login files. Do not move or delete.
