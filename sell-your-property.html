The Sell + Stay Approach

A property that sells itself, literally.

A vacant listing is a hard sell. A property with a live booking calendar and a proven income record is a different conversation entirely.

While it's on the market

Your property can stay under Britsmaid management — bookings, upkeep and guest handling continue. You keep earning through the sale process, if you choose that option below.

For the buyer

Prospective buyers see a property with a real occupancy and revenue history — and can experience it as a guest before they commit. That's a stronger pitch than an empty villa.

How It Works

From listing to sale

  1. Enquiry

    You tell us about the property and your goals for selling.

  2. Valuation conversation

    We review the property's condition, and booking history if it's Britsmaid-managed.

  3. Listed for sale

    The property is marketed for sale, with or without continued short-term bookings.

  4. Buyer enquiries & sale

    Interested buyers view the property; the sale proceeds to completion.
